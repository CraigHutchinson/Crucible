Status: preparation only. No participants or usability/balance conclusions are
recorded. Keep automated event/pixel/replay evidence separate from human reports.

## Session record

With consent, record a pseudonym, date, strategy-game experience, exact build/head,
public OS, physical input/display device, window size, display scale, interruptions
and volunteered accessibility needs. Obtain separate consent for screenshots or
video. Record first-input/completed ticks and assistance. Do not collect names or
unnecessary personal information. A separate hardware session must first pass the
project prerequisite checks; agents without attached verified hardware cannot
claim its receiving.

Keep 2048 samples, quota1780/deadline900, radius8/magnitude4 and the four startup
slots. The simulation starts running immediately. Ask for Space before briefing,
record that tick as a confound, and restart immediately before each attempt.
Allow pause for planning; simulation ticks determine the deadline, not wall time.

## Comparative tasks

1. While paused, ask the player to describe objective, resource recovery and time
   limit before explanation. Ask what a boundary and a directional cue imply.
2. Explain 1 attract, 2 repel, 3 erase, 4 FLOW; Tab selects one of four slots.
   Radial tools place on click. FLOW drags from a start to an endpoint and pushes
   along that direction throughout its capsule, including end caps. It does not
   promise a path, collision constraint or arrival at the endpoint. Space pauses,
   R restarts, F fits, middle drag pans, wheel zooms and Delete erases the selected
   slot. Escape cancels unfinished input, not an accepted queued command.
3. Place attract and repel separately in the same paused slot. Ask which way each
   would move nearby nanites based on the shape alone, then resume to compare.
   Record color reliance, prediction and mistakes before giving corrective hints.
4. Restart, pause and draw a FLOW across the arena. Ask whether white dashed input
   is applied yet. Release, identify queued feedback, then resume and identify
   solid confirmation. Reverse the flow and predict the change. Compare with a
   radial attractor aimed toward the endpoint; record the explanation in their
   words rather than prescribing that the distinction is already clear.
5. While paused, create a new FLOW preview, cancel with Escape, then release. Ask
   what should happen. Repeat with outside release, change slot, replace and erase.
   Record accidental admissions or ambiguity. Do not force a 64-command overflow
   manually; use automated receiving for that capacity edge.
6. Allow fresh unaided attempts using radial tools and FLOW respectively. Alternate
   attempt order across participants where practical; record order, first action,
   pause/timing, edits/slots, outcome/tick and reserve. Flow is not required to win
   and these two attempts do not establish fair balance. Do not show the reference
   sweep until unaided observations are complete.
7. Pan/zoom/refit while paused. Ask whether width, direction, selection and preview
   remain legible at actual display scale. Ask the player to explain a terminal
   outcome and restart. Capture visible examples only with consent.
