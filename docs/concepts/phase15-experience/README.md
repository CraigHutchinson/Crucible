# Crucible: experience direction and cinematic identity

2026-10-09 · proposed design package · source baseline `f70ce7d6afda687f9adc81a86444a8a245d29937`.

Crucible should feel like commanding a living engineered material. The opening
shows one nanite becoming a purposeful collective; the menu offers a clear next
decision; the mission lets the player shape that material; the result explains
what happened and safely carries progress forward. Production fidelity belongs
to that whole journey, with a striking introduction that does not delay play.

This package extends the [existing game intent](../../game-design.md) for architect
review. It does not replace the central phase plan, dispatch implementation, alter
resource rules or claim that these screens, persistence, skills or animation exist.
Phase14's full-frame and worker performance gates remain independent and open.

## Three selected generated concepts

### Hero: one material, one readable identity

![Generated concept: CRUCIBLE title formed from silver wedge nanites with cyan cores](assets/intro-hero-v1.png)

The inspected wordmark spells **CRUCIBLE** correctly. Visible engineered wedges
form crisp letter silhouettes; their cyan cores remain subordinate to silver
material and controlled light. This is a proposed title treatment, not an existing
official logo, final typography asset or executable screenshot. Production should
author a stable glyph mask and controlled choreography rather than derive geometry
from this raster or rely on a generative image to supply final letter spacing.

### Sequence: purposeful choreography into a calm menu

![Generated six-shot concept storyboard: awakening, converging lanes, assembly, title, hero hold, menu transition](assets/intro-storyboard-v1.png)

Read left to right, then top to bottom. A single wedge wakes, related units follow
lanes, the collective interlocks into letter strokes, the readable title settles,
the camera holds, then opens onto a menu surface. See the
[director's timing, lighting and sound brief](director-brief.md). This sheet is six
generated stills; it supplies no animation, interpolation, native rendering or
listening evidence. Shot06's dark blank panel reserves menu composition; the
separate UI board below specifies the proposed controls. Its relief and lattice
are more elaborate than the selected flat gameplay slice; they are decorative
cinematic exploration, not traversal rules. Airborne decorative intro motion does
not introduce a flying gameplay unit.

### Menu and mission selection: high fidelity with clear decisions

![Generated concept board: main menu and mission selection with readable options and nanite material continuity](assets/menu-missions-v1.png)

The labels and CRUCIBLE spelling were visually checked. Cyan focus, dark text
backing and a small number of decisions make these screens legible. The three
mission names are proposed curriculum labels, not shipped levels. Continue is
shown enabled illustratively; actual enablement must follow verified compatible
save data. The amber preview structure is taller than the preferred low lattice:
the production art specification below takes precedence over that generated detail.

## Read and receive the package

- [Director's brief](director-brief.md): twelve-second sequence, camera, PBR,
  light, audio, skip/reduced-motion policies and bounded fidelity tiers.
- [Experience design](experience-design.md): full player journey, missions,
  progression/skills, candidate Quantum mechanics, accessibility and save rules.
- [Receiving plan](receiving-plan.md): proposed next sprint, named consumers,
  agent ownership, dependencies, independent review and acceptance evidence.
- [Provenance](provenance.md): exact prompts, tool output identities, PNG hashes,
  inspection results and remaining risks.

The first next-sprint core is **menu → existing mission → explained result →
persisted mission-boundary progression → safe continue after relaunch**. A separate
bounded cinematic prototype explores high fidelity without blocking that loop.
Full mid-mission restoration, new economy rules, Quantum powers, 3D traversal and
automatic promotion to a new renderer remain later work with their own consumers.
